# Privacy Policy for Roski Wallpapers

**Last Updated:** September 15, 2026

Welcome to **Roski Wallpapers**, developed by **Roski Studio** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

Roski Wallpapers is designed as a free wallpaper and personalization app. 

- **No Personal Data Collected**: We do not collect or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts).
- **No User Accounts**: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use Roski Wallpapers.
- **Local Storage**: Your wallpaper favorites, application cache, and customization preferences (such as Dark/Light theme mode and Auto-Wallpaper schedule) are stored entirely on your local device using Android's internal database (Room) and SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To provide high-resolution content, reliable performance, and monetization, our app integrates trusted third-party SDKs:

#### A. Google AdMob (Advertising)
We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:
- Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID).
- Approximate location (network-derived).
- Interaction with advertisements and ad performance metrics.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps, please visit:  
[https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites]

#### B. Google Firebase (Content Delivery & Analytics)
We use Google Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage to host and deliver wallpaper collections to your device. We may use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated diagnostic metrics (such as app crashes, device models, and feature usage) to improve app performance and stability.

---

### 3. Device Permissions

Roski Wallpapers requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:
- **SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS**: To set selected wallpapers on your Home screen, Lock screen, or both.
- **INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: To load wallpaper catalogs and images from cloud storage, and to respect the "Wi-Fi only" setting.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional)**: If enabled, to alert you when daily wallpaper drops arrive.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Roski Wallpapers does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take appropriate steps.
